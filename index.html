How it installs

Five parts, no soldering, no extra hardware

  1. Laser engineMakes the light, out of sight
  2. Transfer fiberA dark cable that lets you keep the engine up to 200 ft from the light
  3. Safety FiberLinkJoins the dark cable to your glowing fiber
  4. Fiber optic cableGlows along its length, cut to fit
  5. Mirrored end capFinishes the run

Common questions

Is laser fiber optic lighting safe?

Yes, when you follow the instructions. Jagoteq products are FDA and ETL certified, and the glowing fiber has zero electricity and zero heat. Safety training is required, in person or online.

Can it go in water?

The glowing fiber is 100% immersible because there’s no electricity in it. Keep the engine and the ends of the dark transfer fiber out of the water. For outdoor jobs, choose the weather-resistant LF40 IP.

Does it work with DMX?

Yes. The LF120 takes 5-pin DMX and e-DMX (Art-Net/sACN). The LF40 takes 5-pin DMX and e-DMX, with wireless DMX and Bluetooth on request.

Is there a warranty?

Yes. The LF40 has two years on workmanship and parts.
